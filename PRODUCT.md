# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Beginner and mid-level content creators and educators who film themselves talking (YouTube, Reels, Shorts, TikTok, online courses). Comfortable at CapCut level, not in Premiere or DaVinci. They land on kilnframe.com from a video, a post or a search, on a laptop or a phone, deciding in a few seconds whether this is worth a try. A smaller group already edits in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or FilmCraft and comes for the free Kilnframe plugin.

## Product Purpose

kilnframe.com sells Kilnframe Desktop, a paid standalone editor that turns a raw talking recording into a publish-ready video: cuts silences and retakes, writes animations for what is said, sets picture and sound, makes captions, Shorts, titles and thumbnails. It also points editors to the free Kilnframe plugin (/plugin/). Success for the site: a visitor understands what Desktop does from the first screen and the short film, sees the price and the 30-day trial, and leaves an email until the release (then downloads).

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, warm, plain-spoken. A maker's tool from one person who uses it himself (Erhan Meydan, the voice of the films). Kilnframe's clay orange is the accent; paper and navy grounds already set by the site. Confident without hype.

## Anti-references

- "Magic AI" landing pages: gradient text, sparkles, glowing orbs, hero metrics, credit counters.
- SaaS template grids: three identical icon cards, eyebrow labels over every section, pricing tables with ten rows of checkmarks.
- Claims the product cannot back (no competitor bashing, no invented numbers).

## Design Principles

1. Show the real product: the app's own screens and the films made from it, never mockups.
2. One idea per section, said in a line a stranger understands.
3. Price and trial are stated plainly, once, where the decision happens.
4. Privacy is a feature: say where the files stay.
5. English and Turkish pages stay in step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible focus, captions or transcript available for the films, reduced-motion respected, usable at 375 px wide.
