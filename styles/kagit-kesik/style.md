# kagit-kesik

A handmade paper cut-out world: flat layers of matte colored paper stacked on a cream sheet, each layer
casting a soft little shadow, with fine paper fibers and slightly irregular cut edges. Warm, tactile, crafted.
Reference stills: `stills/a-metafor.png`, `stills/b-veri.png`, `stills/c-surec.png`.

## Look

- Cream background sheet. Paper colors: ochre (the highlight), olive, dusty blue, a deeper blue only for inner
  layers seen through cut-outs, and a light paper tone for documents and icons.
- Flat front view, no perspective, no depth of field. Depth comes only from stacking and shadows.
- Every layer has a soft drop shadow; higher layers cast slightly bigger shadows. Shadows stay consistent in
  every scene (the C still is flatter than it should be; follow A).
- Fine paper fiber texture on every layer (seeded SVG turbulence), edges very slightly irregular.
- Cut-outs are part of the language: a hole in the top layer reveals a darker layer below (see the
  microphone grille in A).
- Leave the left third calm for the headline.

## Type

- Nunito Black for headlines and numbers, rendered as cut paper letters with fiber texture and their own
  shadow. Nunito Bold for small labels.
- No dashes in on-screen text.

## Motion

- Things are assembled layer by layer, back to front: each layer slides in from off frame or flips up from
  behind and settles with a small overshoot.
- On accents a layer lifts: its shadow grows and softens, then settles back.
- Loose pieces (ribbons, flags, waves) can sway a little, seeded and small.
- Transition: a big paper sheet slides across the frame and becomes the next background. No fades.

## User images

Images are cut from paper: an irregular cut edge mask, a soft paper drop shadow and a slight lift
between layers, arriving with the style's paper slide. Photos get a gentle warm tint toward the palette.
Screenshots sit on a paper card held by a paper clip, the discussed part marked with a cut paper strip.
Logos are placed on a paper tag without changing their colours.

## Sound palette

Paper slide and shuffle, soft card tap when a layer lands, light rustle for sways, paper whoosh for
transitions.

## Forbidden

Gradients, gloss, outlines, drawn lines, photographic lighting, perspective, depth of field, textures other
than fine paper fiber, fades.
