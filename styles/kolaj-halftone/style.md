# kolaj-halftone

A graphic zine collage: torn pieces of red, blue and newsprint paper taped onto a clean sheet, with simple
pictograms printed in coarse black halftone dots and headlines assembled from letters cut out of different
fonts. Punchy, editorial and handmade. No photographs, ever. Reference stills: `stills/a-metafor.png`,
`stills/b-veri.png`, `stills/c-surec.png`.

## Look

- Smooth newsprint background with faint grain. Paper pieces in muted red, faded blue and a light newsprint
  tone. Black ink for halftone and letters.
- Every element sits on its own torn paper piece: rough seeded torn edge with a thin lighter paper rim, tiny
  shadow, slight rotation. Paper is flat and clean: no wrinkles, creases or stains.
- Objects are simple pictogram silhouettes filled with an even coarse halftone dot pattern (dot size never
  varies to fake light). No photographs, no photorealistic objects.
- Semi-transparent masking tape strips hold pieces down, slightly rotated.
- Leave the left third calm for the headline.

## Type

- Cut-out headlines and numbers: each character (or word) is set in a different font from the set (Archivo
  Black, Anton, Alfa Slab One), printed black or white on its own torn colored paper piece, slightly rotated
  (see "73%" in `b-veri`).
- Small labels in Archivo Bold on a single paper strip.
- No dashes in on-screen text.

## Motion

- Pieces slap onto the page one by one: a very fast scale settle with a tiny rotation. Tape lands a beat
  after the piece it holds.
- On beats, pieces can jump to a slightly different rotation in stepped frames, like stop motion.
- Cut-out letters of a headline land one at a time on the spoken syllables.
- Exit: pieces are pulled off upward. Transition: a large torn sheet is slapped over the frame and becomes
  the next background. No fades.

## User images

Images become collage cut-outs: a torn paper edge mask, a coarse halftone dot treatment in one ink,
strips of tape at the corners, each piece slightly rotated as it slaps on. Screenshots are pasted like a
torn printout with a red pen circle around the discussed part. Logos are taped on without any treatment.

## Sound palette

Paper slap, tape rip and press, short paper tear, quick scissor snip on cut-out letters, paper whoosh for
transitions.

## Forbidden

Photographs, photorealistic shading, modulated halftone that fakes light, wrinkled or stained paper,
gradients, clean vector edges without tearing, fonts outside the set, fades.
