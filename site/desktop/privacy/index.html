Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what the Kilnframe Desktop app and the kilnframe.com website handle, where it goes and what your rights are. The data controller is Erhan Meydan (Kilnframe). You can reach us about anything at hello@kilnframe.com.

In short

  • Your videos and projects stay on your computer. We have no server that sees or keeps them. If you turn on cloud transcription, the sound of the recordings goes to the service you choose (see below).
  • The app has no ads, no usage tracking and no analytics.
  • For AI work, only the text and frames a job needs go to the provider you choose. With a model on your own computer, nothing leaves it.
  • We do not sell your information, use it for ads or share it with others.

What stays on your computer

  • Your recordings, projects, transcripts and exported videos.
  • Speech transcription runs on your computer by default; silence detection and person detection always do, on its own processor or graphics card.
  • Your API keys and your licence key are kept in the system keychain (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, the Linux secret service). They are never sent to us.
  • The app keeps a short log of its latest errors on your computer. It reaches us only when you send an error report.

Cloud transcription (only if you turn it on)

  • Transcription runs on your computer unless you choose a cloud service in Settings. The app asks you before it sends anything the first time.
  • With ElevenLabs, OpenAI or Google chosen, the sound of the recordings being transcribed goes to that service in pieces, as FLAC audio, together with the names in the project's glossary (so that they are spelled right). The video, the project file and your other files are not sent.
  • It goes with your own API key for that service and is subject to that service's privacy terms. With OpenAI or Google, a small timing model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and runs on your computer.

AI features

  • When you start an AI job, the text and the frames that job needs (transcript, notes, small stills) go to the model and provider you chose in Settings, with your own key or account. Whole videos and sound are not sent.
  • With a model on your own computer (a local server), nothing leaves it.
  • Sound effects and music made with ElevenLabs send the text prompt for them. Music and narration made with Google Gemini send the prompt or the text to be read.

Downloads and update checks

  • Models (speech, timing, voice isolation, picture search, face detection) are downloaded from Hugging Face or from our releases on GitHub the first time you start a feature that needs them, never by themselves.
  • The optional sound pack is downloaded from our releases on GitHub when you ask for it.
  • If Claude Code or Codex is installed, the app asks the npm registry for their latest version number, to tell you when an update is out.
  • On macOS and Linux the app asks GitHub for a new version at start and every few hours. On macOS it downloads one only when you agree; on Linux it shows a link to the download page. On Windows the Microsoft Store keeps the app up to date.

Error reports and feedback

  • Nothing is sent by itself. A report goes only after the window has shown you exactly what will be sent and you press Send.

The kilnframe.com website

  • The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses while serving it.
  • Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies: the page opened, where the visit came from, the browser and the screen size. Nothing that identifies you, and no IP address, is kept.
  • If you join the newsletter, your email address is kept with Kit (kit.com). You can leave at any time with the link in every email or by writing to us.
  • When you press Send on the feedback page, your message reaches us by email through Web3Forms.

Why we handle it

We handle information only to do something you asked for (an AI job, a purchase, an update), to check your licence and to support you. This rests on our contract with you and on the actions you start. The newsletter is sent only with your consent.

Transfers abroad

Most of the services above (the AI providers, the transcription services, ElevenLabs, Polar, GitHub, Microsoft, Hugging Face, npm, Kit, Web3Forms) run outside Türkiye, mostly in the United States and the European Union. Information goes to them only during an action you start, and only as much as that action needs.

Keeping and deleting

You can delete what is on your computer at any time: delete a project, remove a key in Settings or uninstall the app. We keep error reports and emails we receive for at most two years after the matter is settled; write to us if you would like them deleted sooner. Your purchase records are kept by Polar for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Türkiye's personal data law (KVKK, Law No. 6698), you have the right to know what is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its use and to take it to another service. Write to hello@kilnframe.com; we answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Kilnframe is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version on this page with a new date. We also announce an important change in the app.
