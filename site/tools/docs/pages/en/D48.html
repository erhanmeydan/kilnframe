Your videos and projects stay on your computer. This page lists what leaves the app, when, and where to; the privacy policy is the full text.

What stays on your computer

  • Your recordings, projects, transcripts and exported videos. Kilnframe has no server that sees or keeps them.
  • The transcript is read on your computer unless you choose a cloud service. Pause cutting, person finding, voice isolation, echo reduction, face finding and picture search always run here.
  • Your API keys and your licence key, in the system keychain (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, the Linux secret service).
  • The app has no ads, no usage tracking and no analytics.

Problem reports

Nothing is sent by itself. Report a problem shows you What will be sent before you press Send: your computer, the app's version, the last errors and the app's last steps, without file names or keys (Report a problem).
