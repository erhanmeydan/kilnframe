Somewhere in your footage there is a shot of a laptop on a desk. Type that, and Kilnframe finds it, on your own computer.

Get it ready, once

  1. Open itIn the Media tab, press Find in Footage.
  2. Download the modelSearching by picture needs a model that is downloaded once and runs on this computer. The window says its size; press Download.
  3. Prepare the filesPrepare reads your recordings and shots. The window counts how many are ready to search.

Search

Type in Describe the shot: a laptop on a desk. The Shots found list each moment with its file and times.

  • For a moment in your recording, Enter plays it in the timeline.
  • For a shot, Put on timeline (or Enter) places it at the playhead.

Nothing found? Try other words, or prepare the remaining files. A file that is not on the disk is not searched; the window names it.

To let your AI model place your shots over the talk, see B-roll.
