One long video holds several shorts. Kilnframe finds them, edits each into a tall clip of its own, and leaves the choice to you.

Find them

In Publish › Short clips, press Find Short Clips. Your AI model edits short videos of 20 to 60 seconds out of the long one: sentences are taken from anywhere in the video and the strongest is put first.

Each one shows its length, its number of sentences, and a score for how well it holds a viewer on its own.

Keep the ones you like

Save as Sequence makes a clip a tall sequence of this project, listed under Sequences in the Media tab. Open Sequence opens it: it is a timeline like any other, to trim, caption and frame.

Make it ready

  • Picture › Follow the person keeps you in the tall frame (Picture).
  • Captions are made for it like for any sequence (Captions).
  • Export with YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels or TikTok as the size. Shorts can be at most 3 minutes long (Export).
