Filmed in front of a green or blue screen? Take the colour away and put anything behind you.

Remove the background

  1. Turn it onSelect the clip, open Clip › Green screen and tick Remove the background colour.
  2. Pick the colourPick from the Picture, then click the screen behind you in the viewer. Or set Colour by hand.
  3. Fine-tuneRange: how much of the colour goes. Soft edge: how soft the edge around you is. Remove colour spill takes away the green glow on hair and shoulders.

What goes behind

Behind the green screen:

  • A colour;
  • A picture from Media;
  • Track below: whatever is on the track under it. For the recording, put a video or a picture on V0; with nothing there, the frame's black shows behind you.
