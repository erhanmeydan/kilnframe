GUIDES

Record screen and camera

Kilnframe records your screen, your camera and your microphone itself, so a lesson goes straight from recording to editing with the camera already paired.

Start a recording

  1. Open the recorderFile › Record…, or Record in the Start Window.
  2. Choose what to recordWhat to record: a screen or a window. Camera and Microphone, or None; the camera preview and the microphone level show they work.
  3. The computer's soundTick Also record the computer's sound (on its own track) for the sound of the apps you show. On a Mac this needs macOS 14.2 or later.
  4. OptionsFrame rate, Hide Kilnframe while recording, Count down 3 seconds and Record clicks too.
  5. RecordPress Record.

While it records

A small recording control sits in the corner of the screen: Pause, Resume and Stop. You can also stop from the recording icon in the menu bar or tray. The take opens in the editor, the camera paired with the screen and the pointer's path kept for the mouse highlight (Screen tutorials).

Permissions

  • Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording for the screen, and Camera and Microphone. macOS asks to reopen Kilnframe after you allow the screen; Quit and Reopen does it. To record clicks, allow Input Monitoring too.
  • Windows: Settings › Privacy & security › Camera and Microphone must let desktop apps use them.
  • Linux: clicks cannot be recorded on a Wayland desktop, and need xinput elsewhere; where the pointer is is still recorded.

If a permission is missing, the window says which one and offers Open Settings.
