FAQ

Short answers, with a link where there is more.

AI

Do I pay extra for the AI?

Not if you have a Claude or ChatGPT subscription: sign in with it and Kilnframe uses it (how). Otherwise you enter an API key and pay its provider for what you use, or run a model on your own computer for free. Kilnframe sells no credits.

Does it work without an AI model?

Yes. The transcript, cutting pauses and filler words, the timeline, colour by hand, sound and export all work without one. Finding retakes, writing animations, AI colour and the other writing jobs need a model.

Privacy

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your videos and projects stay on your computer. Only the text and the small frames an AI job needs go to the model you chose. See How Kilnframe uses AI and the privacy policy.

Which languages does it understand?

The app speaks English and Turkish. The transcript is read by a speech model that knows many languages, and it tells you which one it heard. Captions can be translated into other languages.

Trial and licence

What happens when the trial ends?

Your projects still open and you can keep editing them. Export and AI work need a licence (Licence and trial).

On how many computers can I use it?

Two. Free one with Remove This Computer in the Licence window to move the licence to another.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days, no questions asked. Write to hello@kilnframe.com.

Computers

Which computers does it run on?

Mac with Apple silicon, Windows (x64 and Arm, from the Microsoft Store) and Linux (x64 and Arm64). 16 GB of memory is recommended (Install).

Do I need a graphics card?

No. If you have one (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel or Apple), exports use it and run several times faster.

Other editors

I edit in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve. Is this for me?

Kilnframe Desktop is an editor of its own, and its keys and layout can follow the program you know. If you would rather stay where you are, the free, open-source Kilnframe plugin brings the animations and the music into Premiere Pro, Resolve and FilmCraft.
